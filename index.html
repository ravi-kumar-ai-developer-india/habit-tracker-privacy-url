Privacy Policy

Simple Habit Tracker

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Thank you for using Simple Habit Tracker. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. Because our app is built strictly for students to track daily habits (Exercise, Study, Play, and Sleep), we believe in complete data minimalism and local-first transparency.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Simple Habit Tracker operates entirely offline on your device:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to sign up, log in, or create an account.
  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or school details.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not track your usage, behavior, or app telemetry.

2. Data Storage & Local Database

All habit logs, completion statuses, and daily goals are stored strictly on your device using a secure local SQLite database (Room). Your data never leaves your device, is never transmitted to external servers, and is never shared with third parties.

3. Permissions

Simple Habit Tracker requires zero dangerous permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or device storage.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our app is designed for students of all ages. Since we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, we do not knowingly collect or solicit data from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out via our GitHub repository.
